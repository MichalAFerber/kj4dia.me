Radios have been my passion since I joined the [Civil Air Patrol](https://www.gocivilairpatrol.com/){: target="_blank" } in 1991. I love exploring technology and helping with emergency communications. I used to operate packet radio in the CAP. Now I am into D-STAR and DMR. I have a lot of experience in Emergency Communications for Healthcare, collaborating with SC DHEC, SC EMD, and SCHEART.
